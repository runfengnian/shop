About Us

The brand owner of Runfengnian, Nanjing Kairun Agri-tech Co., Ltd., focuses on the research, development, and production of precooked pork intestine products. We inherit traditional craftsmanship and combine it with modern food processing technology to provide consumers with high-quality and convenient pork intestine products.

We insist on using high-quality raw materials and strictly implement food safety standards. Our 6-step washing process ensures the products are clean and hygienic. Our innovative flash-freezing technology maximizes the preservation of the original flavor and nutrition of the ingredients.

Our goal is to make the traditional pork intestine preparation process simpler and more convenient, allowing every consumer to easily cook restaurant-quality delicacies at home.

Cooking Guide

How to prepare Boiled Pork Intestines

  1. No need to thaw, ready to use upon opening
  2. Can be stir-fried with chili, onion, etc.
  3. Can also be boiled in hot pot or spicy soup
  4. Add seasonings according to personal taste
  5. Heat for 3-5 minutes before eating

How to prepare Spiced Braised Pork Intestines

  1. Ready to eat after opening
  2. Better flavor after heating
  3. Can be served over rice or noodles as a topping
  4. Can also be used as an ingredient for stir-fries and stews
  5. Microwave for 1-2 minutes

Tips

  • The product is seasoned, adjust salt according to personal taste
  • Unopened products should be stored according to packaging instructions
  • Consume as soon as possible after opening
  • Recommended to eat with vegetables for a more balanced nutrition
