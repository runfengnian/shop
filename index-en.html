Our Services

Connecting Premium Domestic Meat with Global Dining Tables

Nanjing Kairun Agri-tech Co., Ltd. was founded in 2015, specializing in Global supply chain services for frozen meat.

We are highly experienced in import and export business, with strong capabilities in integrating upstream and downstream resources. Upstream, we connect with domestic high-quality meat slaughtering and processing plants, covering chicken, pork, beef, and mutton products. Downstream, we directly serve overseas food purchasers.

Having been deeply rooted in the domestic meat supply chain for decades, we are fully familiar with the processing and characteristics of domestic meat. Whatever your specific application scenario and requirements may be, we provide the solution.
